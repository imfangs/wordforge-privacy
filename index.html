一句话概括

单词合合乐 是一款以本地内容和本地存储为主的儿童英语学习游戏。 我们不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人信息。

我们收集哪些信息?

不收集任何个人信息。

App 不需要注册账号，不包含分析或广告 SDK，也不会将数据发送给开发者服务器。

数据存储在哪里?

App 会在您的设备本地保存以下游戏进度数据（使用 iOS 系统的 UserDefaults）:

  • 已发现的元素列表
  • 词汇掌握度进度
  • 用户设置（音效、背景音乐、词汇等级偏好等）

这些数据 只保存在您本地设备上，不会上传到任何服务器。卸载 App 时会随之删除。

我们使用哪些系统功能?

  • 触觉反馈（Haptics）：合成成功/失败的震动提示
  • 系统语音朗读（AVSpeechSynthesizer）：朗读英文单词，完全离线，不上传录音
  • 本地音频播放（AVAudioPlayer）：背景音乐和音效
  • 可选语音识别（SFSpeechRecognizer）：仅在用户主动点击麦克风并授权后，用于识别用户说出的英文单词。根据设备和系统情况，音频可能由 Apple 的语音识别服务处理并需要网络连接。开发者不会接收、存储或分享这些音频或识别结果。

购买与恢复购买

从 1.2.0 起，完整词库通过 Apple App Store 提供一次性购买。付款由 Apple 处理；开发者不接收银行卡或账单资料。

App 在设备上读取并验证 Apple 提供的购买和应用交易信息，用于解锁内容、恢复购买及识别旧版本用户权益。这些交易信息和学习记录不会发送到开发者服务器。

购买与恢复购买位于家长验证之后。Apple 对交易信息的处理方式见其 隐私政策。

第三方服务

我们不接入任何第三方 SDK——没有分析、没有广告、没有崩溃收集、没有社交登录。可选语音识别及内购使用 Apple 提供的系统框架和服务；内购通过 StoreKit 接入 App Store。

儿童隐私

本 App 面向儿童用户设计，采用以下隐私措施：

  • 无需注册账号，学习记录保存在设备上，不发送到开发者服务器
  • 不包含广告、分析或社交登录 SDK
  • 购买与恢复购买位于家长验证之后，由 Apple 处理付款

In One Sentence

WordForge (单词合合乐) is a children's English learning game whose core content and progress storage are local to the device. We do not collect, upload, or share any personal information.

What information do we collect?

None.

The app requires no account, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and sends no data to developer-operated servers.

Where is data stored?

The app saves the following game-progress data locally on your device (via iOS UserDefaults):

  • List of discovered elements
  • Vocabulary mastery progress
  • User settings (sound effects, music, vocabulary level filters, etc.)

This data stays on your device only and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Which system features do we use?

  • Haptic feedback: fusion success/failure vibration
  • System speech synthesis (AVSpeechSynthesizer): reads English words aloud, fully offline, no audio uploaded
  • Local audio playback (AVAudioPlayer): background music and sound effects
  • Optional speech recognition (SFSpeechRecognizer): only after the user taps the microphone and grants permission, the feature recognizes English words spoken by the user. Depending on the device and OS, audio may be processed by Apple's speech recognition service and may require a network connection. The developer does not receive, store, or share the audio or recognition results.

Purchases and restoration

Starting with version 1.2.0, full vocabulary access is available through a one-time App Store purchase. Apple processes payments; the developer does not receive payment-card or billing details.

The app reads and verifies Apple-provided purchase and app-transaction information on the device to unlock content, restore purchases and recognise existing-user access. Transaction information and learning records are not sent to developer-operated servers.

Purchases and restoration are behind a parental gate. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles transaction information.

Third-party services

We do not integrate any third-party SDK — no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no social login. Optional speech recognition and in-app purchases use Apple's system frameworks and services; purchases connect to the App Store through StoreKit.

Children's Privacy

This app is designed for children and uses the following privacy practices:

  • No account is required; learning records stay on the device and are not sent to developer-operated servers
  • No advertising, analytics or social-login SDKs
  • Purchases and restoration are behind a parental gate, with payments handled by Apple
